              Quick guide

              Tap a heading to open it. Most numbers update by themselves as you change things.

              Install it as an app (works without internet)
              • iPad / iPhone (Safari): open the site, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
              • Android tablet (Chrome): open the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
              • Laptop (Edge or Chrome): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose Apps → Install this site as an app.
              • It gets its own icon and opens full screen. After the first visit it works with no internet; when you're online it picks up updates by itself (close and reopen it to see them).
              • On an iPad or iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own saved characters, separate from Safari's. To bring a character over, Export it in Safari, then Import that file in the app.
              Saving, switching and printing characters
              • Everything saves automatically in this browser. Each device (laptop, tablet) keeps its own characters.
              • Use the Character list at the top to switch between characters; New, Duplicate and Delete are next to it. Type a Name to label one.
              • To move a character to another device: Export saves a file; on the other device, Import that file.
              • Print makes a one-page character sheet.
              • After an update, reload the page to get the newest version.
              Dice rolls
              • Any 🎲 Roll button rolls for you: saves, skills, ability checks, initiative, attacks, damage, spells, healing.
              • The result appears in a box at the bottom of the screen; the last few rolls are kept there.
              • Attack rolls spot natural 20s and 1s and critical threats, roll the confirmation and the extra damage.
              Character tab
              • House rules: switch each one Y or N. Encumbrance (what you carry slows you), Max Healing (healing rolls give their maximum), Action Points (Pathfinder hero points), Flaws (up to two, each gives a bonus feat), Extra Campaign Trait (a third trait).
              • Search finds anything by name: races, classes, archetypes, feats, traits, skills, spells, gear. Tap a result to see it; most have a button to add it (e.g. Take an archetype or trait).
              • Race: tap any item (Medium, Darkvision...) to see what it does. Open Alternate racial traits to swap some traits for others; ones that clash are greyed out.
              • Hit points: use − / + to set an amount (negative for damage), then Apply; Full heals completely.
              • AC, CMD, CMB, saves and combat maneuvers are in the Race card, each with a Roll button.
              • Classes: choose a class for each level (multiclassing works). Under each level in your favored class, choose +1 hit point, +1 skill rank or your race's option.
              • Open a class's section to add archetypes and to see its features by level: tap any feature to read what it does. Struck-out features were replaced by an archetype; coloured ones come from it.
              • Clerics choose two domains and inquisitors a domain or inquisition in their class's section. A druid's Nature bond there is an animal companion or a domain; a domain adds a domain spell each spell level.
              • Ability scores: point buy with − / +; pick where racial +2s go and your ability increases at 4th, 8th... level.
              Skills tab
              • Add ranks with − / +; the count at the top shows how many you have left. Class skills get +3 once they have a rank.
              • For Craft, Perform and Profession, type a specialty (e.g. "alchemy") and press Add.
              Feats tab (feats, traits and flaws)
              • Each feat slot shows the level it comes from; Choose a feat opens a list you can search and filter. Green ✓ / red ✗ show whether you meet each prerequisite.
              • Feats like Weapon Focus or Skill Focus ask which weapon or skill; that choice is applied automatically.
              • Traits: choose two (three with the Extra Campaign Trait house rule). Their bonuses are added for you.
              • Flaws appear when that house rule is on.
              Armor tab
              • Choose armor and a shield, and their Quality (masterwork or +1 to +5). AC, Dex limit, check penalty, spell failure and speed update for you, with warnings if you aren't proficient.
              • Material makes it mithral, adamantine, darkwood, dragonhide and so on (only the ones that item can be made of are listed); price, weight and stats change to match. Dragonhide, darkwood and the leathers are fine for druids.
              • To add special abilities (fortification...), use Add magic gear on the Magic Items tab.
              Weapons tab
              • Pick a weapon in the list below, then Add it. Each weapon shows its attacks, damage and critical with Roll buttons.
              • Quality sets masterwork or +1 to +5; Special abilities (flaming, keen, holy...) add their damage automatically. Situational ones (holy against evil) get their own Roll button.
              • Combat options at the top: Power Attack, Deadly Aim, Rapid Shot (once you have the feats) and two-weapon fighting.
              Spells tab
              • Shows spells per day for each casting class, and the class's spell list. Choose a spell and press Add to my spells (spontaneous casters: add the spells you know).
              • My spells show attack bonus, damage, save DC and healing at your caster level, with Roll buttons and a spell resistance check.
              Magic Items tab
              • Browse by category or search; choose an item to see it, then Add it (pick a version, e.g. +2, if it has one).
              • Add magic gear is for treasure and purchases at market price, no feats needed: make a weapon or armor magic, or add potions, scrolls and wands of any spell.
              • My magic items lists what you own, with − / + for quantities.
              Craft tab
              • For making items yourself with the item creation rules: magic weapons and armor, potions, scrolls, wands, or any listed item (press Craft this item in its details on the Magic Items tab).
              • It checks the feat you need and each requirement (✓ / ✗; tick "I meet this" for ones it can't judge), and shows the Spellcraft DC, your Spellcraft with a Roll button, the cost (half the price) and the time. Rush it halves the time for +5 DC.
              Equipment tab
              • Set your gold, or use your class's starting gold or the wealth for your level.
              • Add gear from the list below. Totals show what you've spent (armor, weapons, magic items and gear) and what's left, plus the weight you carry (and your load, with the Encumbrance house rule).
